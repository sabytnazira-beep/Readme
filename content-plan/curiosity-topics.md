# Curiosity Topics: Marketing & Business Ideas People Want to Know

These are broad topics for business owners, framed as curiosity gaps. Each one makes a surprising claim or asks a "why" question, so people want to know the answer and share it. They sit on top of the segment topics (see `topics-by-segment.md`); rotate your examples across industries.

**Default visual:** Night Depth navy gradient (`#0a1a30 → #16304e → #2a517f`), white headline, and the **key word or number in Steel Teal `#6896a3`**. Save-style explainers use the white or Mist slide style with navy text and `#2a517f` labels. Any "wrong" or "myth" element is shown in white at 40% opacity with a teal strike-through, never red.

> Psychology effects are real, named findings. Present them as tendencies ("often", "usually"), not guarantees. Numbers marked *e.g.* are examples.
