# Reel Series: "How We Actually Work" (Process Content)

**Ladder level:** mostly PROFILE (10%) and WATCH (20%). This series is what a visitor sees after 3–4 posts and asks: *"Who are these people, and do they actually have a system?"* Pin the overview reel, and put the series in a **Highlight called "Our Process"**.

**The system shown:** Research → Segments → Matrix → Content plan → Shoot plan → Monthly analytics

**Filming rule: show your real work.** The research files, segment files and matrix tables you already have *are* the props. Film real screens, printed tables, sticky-note walls and real shoot days. That is original content, which Instagram rewards in 2026, and it's proof competitors can't fake.

> Blur client names and numbers unless the client has approved showing them.
