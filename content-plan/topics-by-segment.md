# Post Topics by Segment: What They Actually Want to Hear

Every topic comes from a specific finding in [`/research`](../research/00-overview-market-context.md), not from generic marketing advice.

**How to read each row:**
- **Level:** the signal-ladder level (S = Share, V = Save, W = Watch, P = Profile, C = Convert).
- **Why it lands:** the research finding the topic is built on.
- **Visual & colour:** brand direction. Default is a Night Depth navy gradient with white text and Steel Teal `#6896a3` on the key word or number (see [brand guide](../brand/brand-guide.md)).

> Numbers marked *e.g.* are illustrative. All other figures come from the cited research. Check the research file before quoting them.
