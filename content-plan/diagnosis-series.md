# Series: "What's Wrong With Your Business" (Diagnosis Talking-Head Reels)

**Based on:** the competitor format that's working: *"Let me tell you what's wrong with your business. And no, this isn't a guess. It's a pattern."* → problem → the team/system that fixes it → CTA.

## Why the format works
1. **Direct accusation.** "What's wrong with *your* business" makes every owner check whether it's them.
2. **Authority.** "Not a guess, it's a pattern" positions you as someone who has seen hundreds of cases.
3. **Relief.** Showing the full team says "you don't have to do this alone."
4. **Low-effort promise.** "Focus on running your business" is the owner's real wish.

## How to beat the competitor at their own format
- **They name a vague problem** ("lacks strategy, consistency"). **You name one precise pattern per video.** Precise means believable.
- **They list job titles.** **You show your system:** research → segments → matrix → shoot → report in AED. Job titles are something every agency has; a visible system isn't.
- **Make it a numbered series** ("Pattern #1, #2…") so people binge it and follow for the next one.

> Only say "every business we audit" or quote numbers from your own audits if they're true. Otherwise say "a pattern we keep seeing".

| Element | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Framing** | Talking head at desk or in the studio, eye level, cool colour grade. Our strategy wall or monitors softly blurred behind |

| **Captions** | White on a `#0a1a30` bar at 80% opacity. The one key word per line is in Steel Teal |
| **"Wrong" moments** | The phrase at 40% white with a teal strike-through (no red) |
| **List items** (team/system) | Pop in as white chips with a teal dot, one per beat, with a B-roll cut on each |
| **End card** | Night Depth background, white "DM AUDIT", Tide gradient button `#2a517f → #6896a3` with navy text |
| **Ladder level** | Share (hook and pattern) → Convert (the ending). One per week, pinned as a series in Highlights |

## Episode scripts

### #1 · The one-person marketing department
- **Hook (spoken + on-screen):** "Let me tell you what's wrong with your marketing. It's not a guess. It's a pattern."
- **Pattern:** "You've hired one person and asked them to be the photographer, editor, copywriter, strategist, ad manager and analyst."
- **Why it fails:** "That's not a job. That's a department. So they do everything a little, and nothing properly."
- **Fix / us:** "We bring the department: strategist, photographer and videographer, editor, copywriter, designer, ad manager. And one system connecting all of them, from research to the monthly report."
- **CTA:** "You run the business. We run the growth. DM AUDIT."
- **B-roll:** one person juggling 6 apps → a cut to our team at a shoot, each role as a teal chip.

### #2 · Content for everyone
- **Hook:** "What's wrong with your business? Your content is for everyone."
- **Pattern:** "You post for 'our customers'. But a daily regular, a tourist and a corporate client want completely different things."
- **Why it fails:** "When a post is for everyone, nobody feels it's for them. So nobody stops scrolling."
- **Fix / us:** "Before we plan a single post, we research your market and split your customers into segments. Every post speaks to one of them."
- **CTA:** "Want to see your segments? DM AUDIT."
- **B-roll:** a crowd blurred → one person in focus with a teal outline; our segment cards on the desk.

### #3 · Posting without a reason
- **Hook:** "You don't have a posting problem. You have a *reason* problem."
- **Pattern:** "Most businesses post because 'we haven't posted in a while'."
- **Why it fails:** "A post without a job is a letter without an address. It doesn't arrive anywhere."
- **Fix / us:** "Every post we make has one job: get shared, get saved, get watched, get a profile visit, or get a message. We decide that before we film."
- **CTA:** "DM AUDIT and we'll show you which jobs your posts are missing."
- **B-roll:** an envelope with no address in the bin → our matrix table filling in.

### #4 · Reading the wrong numbers
- **Hook:** "Let me guess. Your marketing report is full of likes."
- **Pattern:** "Likes, followers, views. It looks great in a report."
- **Why it fails:** "None of those pay rent. Most owners can't answer one question: what did marketing bring in this month, in AED?"
- **Fix / us:** "Our monthly report starts with customers, bookings and cost, then tells you what we're changing next month."
- **CTA:** "If you can't answer that question, DM AUDIT."
- **B-roll:** a report with "likes" crossed out in teal → a report with customers, revenue and cost highlighted.

### #5 · Leads die in WhatsApp
- **Hook:** "Your marketing works. Your WhatsApp doesn't."
- **Pattern:** "People message you, and someone replies three hours later."
- **Why it fails:** "In the UAE people message three or four businesses at once. The fastest reply usually wins."
- **Fix / us:** "We don't just bring the leads. We set up the reply, the follow-up and the booking link so they don't leak."
- **CTA:** "DM AUDIT. We'll check how many you're losing."
- **B-roll:** a chat with a "Seen 3h" timestamp → a 2-minute reply with a teal ✓.

### #6 · Copying trends
- **Hook:** "What's wrong with your business? You look like everyone else."
- **Pattern:** "Your team copies every trend: same audio, same format, same captions."
- **Why it fails:** "Copying keeps you current. It also makes you forgettable."
- **Fix / us:** "We give your team a filter: does it fit your voice, can you add your twist, would you be embarrassed if a competitor posted it? Then we make the content trends can't copy: your people and your story."
- **CTA:** "DM AUDIT."
- **B-roll:** 6 identical reels in a grid → one distinct reel with a teal frame.

### #7 · One big shoot, then silence
- **Hook:** "You spent a lot on one beautiful shoot. Then you went quiet for two months."
- **Pattern:** "Big launch, big video, then nothing."
- **Why it fails:** "Instagram rewards consistency. One hero video doesn't feed a whole month."
- **Fix / us:** "We plan a month from every shoot day: hero content, series and everyday posts, so you never run out."
- **CTA:** "DM AUDIT for a month-from-one-shoot plan."
- **B-roll:** a calendar with one filled day → the same calendar filling up with teal dots.

### #8 · People love you in person
- **Hook:** "Your customers love you in person. Online, they have no idea why."
- **Pattern:** "Your team, your service, your owner: that's why people come back. Your feed shows products and empty rooms."
- **Why it fails:** "People don't follow logos. They follow people."
- **Fix / us:** "We put your people on camera, without scripts and without awkwardness."
- **CTA:** "DM AUDIT."
- **B-roll:** an empty shop shot (40%) → a staff member laughing with a customer (full colour).

### #9 · Ads to a weak profile
- **Hook:** "You're paying for ads that send people to a profile that doesn't convince them."
- **Pattern:** "The ad works. They tap. They see an old feed, no proof, no clear offer, and they leave."
- **Why it fails:** "The ad did its job. The profile didn't."
- **Fix / us:** "Before we spend a dirham on ads, we fix the profile: bio, pinned posts, proof, and one clear action."
- **CTA:** "DM AUDIT. We'll review your profile."
- **B-roll:** an ad tap → a scroll through a dull profile → the fixed profile with teal-highlighted pinned posts.

### #10 · You don't know your customer cost
- **Hook:** "Quick question: what does one new customer cost you?"
- **Pattern:** "Most owners don't know. They know what they spend, not what they get."
- **Why it fails:** "If you don't know the number, you can't know if marketing is working."
- **Fix / us:** "We track it from day one and show you it going down."
- **CTA:** "DM AUDIT and we'll work out yours."
- **B-roll:** a price tag with a "?" → "AED 200 → 160 (e.g.)" in teal.

### #11 · Customers come once
- **Hook:** "Your problem isn't getting customers. It's keeping them."
- **Pattern:** "All the budget goes to new people. Nobody talks to the ones who already paid."
- **Why it fails:** "Keeping a customer is usually far cheaper than finding a new one."
- **Fix / us:** "We build the part after the first visit: WhatsApp follow-ups, loyalty, reasons to come back."
- **CTA:** "DM AUDIT."
- **B-roll:** people leaving through a door → a teal circular arrow bringing them back.

### #12 · The owner is the bottleneck
- **Hook:** "What's wrong with your business? Every post waits for you."
- **Pattern:** "Nothing goes out until the owner approves it, and the owner is busy running the business."
- **Why it fails:** "Marketing slows down to the speed of your calendar."
- **Fix / us:** "We plan a month ahead, you approve it in one meeting, and we run it. You focus on running the business while we focus on growing the brand."
- **CTA:** "DM AUDIT."
- **B-roll:** a phone full of "waiting for approval" messages → a single calendar approval with a teal ✓.

## Posting plan
- **Weekly:** one episode (series #1–#12 = 3 months).
- **Pin #1** as the series opener, and collect all episodes in a "What's Wrong?" Highlight.
- **Trial Reels:** test two hook versions per episode on non-followers first ("Let me tell you what's wrong with your business" vs "What's wrong with your business? [specific pattern]").
- **Industry versions:** re-cut the strongest episodes with one industry example ("…your café", "…your clinic") to reach specific segments.
