# Agency Reels: Q4 2026 Starter Set (5 Reels)

Based on the [content matrix framework](../content-matrix/00-framework.md). The Q4 focus is **specialty coffee (primary) + restaurants (secondary)**.

**Rules for every reel**
- **The hook's visual and the hook's text say the same thing.** If the text says "strategy," the viewer sees a strategy document.
- **The hook = problem + a promise.** Tell them why to stay to the end.
- **The body = one idea per line, explained simply enough for a child, and every line has its own visual.**
- Name the audience on screen in the first 2 seconds. End with one call to action.

## Reel 1: "You don't need another strategy" · SHARE (40%)
**For:** marketing managers at multi-branch coffee brands · **Signal:** sends + watch time · **Length:** ~35s

### Hook (0–3s)
| | |
|---|---|
| **Visual** | A thick printed strategy deck drops onto a café counter with a *thud*. Next to it, a phone showing an Instagram feed that hasn't posted in 9 days |
| **On-screen text** | "Your coffee brand doesn't need another marketing strategy." |
| **Spoken** | "If you market a coffee brand, you don't need another strategy. You need the thing that's actually missing, and I'll show you what it is." |

### Body
| # | Say (simple) | Show |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "A strategy is like a recipe." | Hand opens a recipe book on the counter |
| 2 | "But a recipe doesn't make the coffee." | Empty cup next to the open book. Nothing happens |
| 3 | "Someone has to grind, pour and serve." | Fast cuts: grinder, espresso pour, cup slides across the bar |
| 4 | "Your team has the recipe. They just don't have enough hands." | One barista alone behind a busy bar, orders piling up (receipt printer spitting tickets) |
| 5 | "That's not a strategy problem. It's about 8 hours a week of production missing." | Clock spins forward; text: "+8 hours / week" |
| 6 | "So we become the extra hands. Same recipe, more coffee served." | Our team walks in with a camera and lights; the feed on the phone fills with new posts |

### End (CTA)
**Text:** "Send this to your marketing team." · **Spoken:** "Send this to the person who runs your content."

**Caption keywords:** specialty coffee marketing, coffee brand Instagram, content production Dubai

## Reel 3: "We gave a café one new drink" · WATCH (20%)
**For:** coffee marketing managers who doubt outsourcing · **Signal:** watch time + completion · **Length:** ~50s

### Hook (0–3s)
| | |
|---|---|
| **Visual** | A blank 14-day calendar on a wall. Someone pins a single drink photo to day 1 |
| **On-screen text** | "One new drink → two weeks of content. Watch." |
| **Spoken** | "A coffee brand gave us one new drink. Here's how we'd turn it into two full weeks of content, and what happens on day 14." |

### Body (the calendar fills progressively; that's the retention)
| # | Say (simple) | Show |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Day 1: we plan. Every day gets a job." | Sticky notes go on each calendar day: "hero", "BTS", "barista"… |
| 2 | "Day 2: one shoot. Everything filmed in 4 hours." | Time-lapse shoot; counter "0 → 24 clips" |
| 3 | "Days 3–5: tease. People see it before they can buy it." | Blurry drink reel, then text "Coming Friday" |
| 4 | "Day 6: launch. The hero video goes out." | Calendar day 6 lights up; hero reel plays |
| 5 | "Days 7–12: stories from real people." | Barista clip, customer sip, creator visit, one per day |
| 6 | "Day 13: last chance." | Text: "Last 2 days" on the cup |
| 7 | "Day 14: we check what sold, not what got likes." | Report screen: drinks sold and code redemptions, likes crossed out |

### End (CTA)
**Text:** "Part 2: the results → follow." · **Spoken:** "Follow. Next reel shows what the numbers looked like."

> Use real client results in part 2 when you have them. Don't present invented numbers as real.

## Reel 5: "Your most expensive video might be your worst one" · SHARE → PROFILE
**For:** coffee and restaurant founders who paid for high-end production · **Signal:** sends + comments, then profile visits · **Length:** ~40s

### Hook (0–3s)
| | |
|---|---|
| **Visual** | Split screen. Left: a big cinema camera rig with a price tag, "AED 40,000". Right: a phone on a small tripod, "AED 0" |
| **On-screen text** | "Your most expensive video might be your worst-performing one." |
| **Spoken** | "Paid a lot for a beautiful video and sales didn't move? It wasn't wasted money, it was the wrong job. Here's how to fix it." |

### Body
| # | Say (simple) | Show |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Every video has a job, like every tool in a toolbox." | A toolbox opens: hammer, screwdriver, tape measure |
| 2 | "A beautiful film is a hammer: great for your website, ads and launches." | Hero film playing on a laptop and website mock-up |
| 3 | "But Instagram mostly wants a screwdriver: real, quick, human." | Barista phone clip, shaky and honest, getting hearts |
| 4 | "Using a hammer for a screw doesn't work, however expensive the hammer." | Hammer hitting a screw, bending it (a funny moment) |
| 5 | "So we split it: a little hero, a lot of series, the rest native." | Pie chart builds: 10% hero / 40% series / 50% native |
| 6 | "Right tool, right job. That's how content turns into sales." | Till screen: "Order #247" ticket prints |

### End (CTA)
**Text:** "How we plan this → see our profile." · **Spoken:** "Want to see how we plan this for coffee brands? It's pinned on our profile."
