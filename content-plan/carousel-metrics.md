# Carousel: "Every business needs to know if its marketing is actually working"

**Ladder level:** SAVE (with a strong share pull: "send to your partner / marketing team")
**Audience:** owners and founders across all segments who pay a marketing team or agency
**Format:** 10 slides · 4:5 · 1080 × 1350 px
**Style:** based on the reference carousel (minimal navy cover, one line running across the slides, a branch "map" slide, then numbered white explainer slides with bad-vs-good line illustrations), in our brand colours ([brand guide](../brand/brand-guide.md)).

> The AED figures are examples. Label them "e.g." in the design.

---

## Design system (applies to every slide)

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| **Canvas** | 1080 × 1350 px, 96 px side margins, 80 px top/bottom |
| **Font** | Clean grotesk sans (Inter or Helvetica Now) until the brand font is confirmed. Headlines Bold, body Regular |
| **Top bar** | Left: `@yourhandle` (22 px). Right: page counter `1/10 ⟶` (22 px) |
| **Bottom right** | "Save for later 🔖" pill. **Navy slides:** white pill, `#0a1a30` text. **White slides:** `#0a1a30` pill, white text |
| **Bottom centre** | 10 pagination dots. Active dot white (navy slides) or `#0a1a30` (white slides); inactive at 40% opacity |
| **Swipe arrow** | 56 px circle at the right edge, vertically centred: `#6896a3` fill with a `#0a1a30` chevron |
| **Navy slides** (1, 2, 9, 10) | **Night Depth** gradient `#0a1a30 → #16304e → #2a517f` at 160°. White text |
| **White slides** (3–8) | White `#ffffff` background (or Mist `#ffffff → #e8eff1`). Text `#0a1a30` |
| **Number tag** (`#1`–`#6`) | Solid `#0a1a30` block, white bold text, 40 px tall, sits above the heading, as in the reference |
| **Illustrations** | Simple line art, 3 px stroke. **Bad side:** `#0a1a30` at 40% opacity (grey look). **Good side:** Steel Teal `#6896a3` lines |
| **Illustration labels** | Bad: `#0a1a30` at 60%. Good: `#2a517f` bold (8.1:1 on white). *Don't use teal for small text on white (3.2:1).* |
| **No red** | The reference uses red for "good". We use teal lines and an Ocean Blue label instead |
| **Colour ratio** | About 70% navy / 20% white / 10% teal across the whole carousel |

**The continuous line.** A 4 px white line starts in the middle of slide 1, right after the headline, and runs off the right edge. It comes back in from the left edge of slide 2 and splits into 6 branches. On slides 3–8, a thin 2 px `#6896a3` line runs along the bottom edge from left to right, so the carousel feels like one continuous piece and invites the next swipe.

## Slide 1: Cover (navy)
- **Text (centre-left, 64 px, white, Regular):** "Every business needs to know"
- **Design:** the white line leaves the end of the text and runs to the right edge. The swipe arrow circle sits where the line meets the edge, as in the reference. Lots of empty space; nothing else on the slide.
- **Why:** the sentence is unfinished, so viewers have to swipe to read the rest.

## Slide 2: The map (navy)
- **Top text (white, 40 px, left-aligned above the fan):** "…if their marketing team is doing a good job."
- **Design:** the line comes in from the left edge and splits into 6 branches, each ending in a small dot with a white label (34 px):
  1. Cost per customer
  2. Enquiries → customers
  3. Reply time
  4. Repeat customers
  5. Shares, not likes
  6. Reports in AED
- The branch dots are Steel Teal `#6896a3`, so the eye goes to the six topics.

## Slide 3: `#1` Cost per customer (white)
- **Heading (bold, 52 px, navy):** Cost per customer
- **Body (26 px, navy):**
  "How much you spend to win one new customer.
  A good team knows this number, and it goes down over time. If nobody can tell you the number, that's your answer."
- **e.g. line (22 px, navy 60%):** e.g. AED 10,000 spent ÷ 50 new customers = AED 200 each
- **Illustration:** Left, "Bad sign": a price tag with a question mark. Right, "Good sign": a price tag with a teal downward arrow and "AED 200 → 160".

## Slide 4: `#2` Enquiries → customers (white)
- **Heading:** Enquiries → customers
- **Body:**
  "Messages are nice. Paying customers are the job.
  A good team tracks how many enquiries actually book, buy or visit, not just how many messages came in."
- **e.g.:** e.g. 100 enquiries → 20 bookings = 20%
- **Illustration:** Left, "Bad sign": a wide funnel full of chat bubbles with nothing coming out the bottom. Right, "Good sign": the same funnel with teal calendar ticks coming out.

## Slide 5: `#3` Reply time (white)
- **Heading:** Reply time
- **Body:**
  "In the UAE, people message 3–4 businesses at once. The fastest reply usually wins.
  A good team replies in minutes, not hours."
- **e.g.:** Aim for under 5 minutes during opening hours
- **Illustration:** Left, "Bad sign": a clock showing 3 hours and a chat bubble with "Seen". Right, "Good sign": a teal stopwatch at 2 minutes and a teal ✓ on a chat bubble.

## Slide 6: `#4` Repeat customers (white)
- **Heading:** Repeat customers
- **Body:**
  "Winning a new customer is expensive. Keeping one is cheap.
  A good team brings people back, not just in once."
- **e.g.:** e.g. 100 first-timers → 35 come back = 35%
- **Illustration:** Left, "Bad sign": a door with people walking out in a straight line. Right, "Good sign": a teal circular arrow bringing people back through the door.

## Slide 7: `#5` Shares, not likes (white)
- **Heading:** Shares, not likes
- **Body:**
  "A like is a nod. A share is a recommendation.
  Instagram shows shared posts to more new people. A good team makes content people send to friends."
- **Illustration:** Left, "Bad sign": a grey heart icon with a small "+1". Right, "Good sign": a teal paper-plane icon flying to 3 small phones.

## Slide 8: `#6` Reports in AED (white)
- **Heading:** Reports in AED
- **Body:**
  "Followers and views are clues, not results.
  A good team's monthly report answers one question: what did marketing bring in, and what did it cost?"
- **Illustration:** Left, "Bad sign": a report sheet showing "Views ↑ Followers ↑ Likes ↑" in grey. Right, "Good sign": a report sheet showing "Customers, Revenue, Cost" with a teal highlight on the bottom line.

## Slide 9: The scorecard (navy)
- **Heading (white, 48 px):** Your team is doing a good job if they can answer these:
- **Checklist (white, 28 px; each item with a Steel Teal ✓ in a circle):**
  - What does one new customer cost us?
  - How many enquiries became customers?
  - How fast do we reply?
  - How many customers came back?
  - Which posts got shared?
  - What did marketing bring in this month, in AED?
- **Bottom line (Steel Teal `#6896a3`, 30 px bold, 5.4:1 on navy):** "Can't answer? That's the problem."

## Slide 10: CTA (navy)
- **Heading (white, 56 px):** Not sure what your numbers are?
- **Subline (white, 28 px, 80% opacity):** We'll find them with you in a free 15-minute Growth Audit.
- **Button:** a Tide gradient pill `#2a517f → #6896a3` with `#0a1a30` bold text: **DM "AUDIT"**
- **Small icons row (white line icons):** 🔖 Save · ✈️ Send to your business partner
- **Design:** a Glow spotlight (radial `#6896a3` at 30%) behind the button. The white line from slide 1 comes back in from the left and ends in a dot beside the button, closing the loop.
