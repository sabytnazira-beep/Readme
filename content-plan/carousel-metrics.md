# Carousel: "Every business needs to know these 7 numbers"

**Ladder level:** SAVE (with a strong share pull) · **Audience:** owners and marketing managers across all segments · **Format:** 10 slides, 4:5

**Design rules:** one big number or word per slide, one simple sentence, one AED example. Use the same layout on every slide so people swipe fast. Slides 2–8 are numbered "1/7" to "7/7" so viewers feel progress and finish the carousel.

> Example numbers are illustrative. Label them "example" in the design.

---

### Slide 1: Cover
- **Headline:** Every business needs to know these 7 numbers.
- **Subline:** Most only check one: likes.
- **Visual:** A phone showing a heart icon with a big red ✕, and 7 blank number tiles below it.

### Slide 2: 1/7 · Cost per customer
- **Headline:** What does one new customer cost you?
- **Simple line:** Everything you spent on marketing, divided by the number of new customers it brought in.
- **Example:** AED 10,000 spent → 50 new customers = **AED 200 per customer**
- **Visual:** A price tag hanging on a customer icon.

### Slide 3: 2/7 · Customer lifetime value
- **Headline:** How much is one customer worth over time?
- **Simple line:** Not their first visit. Everything they spend with you over the whole time they stay a customer.
- **Example:** AED 150 per visit × 2 visits a month × 12 months = **AED 3,600**
- **Visual:** One coffee cup multiplying into a stack of 24.

### Slide 4: 3/7 · The golden ratio
- **Headline:** Value ÷ cost. Aim for at least 3 to 1.
- **Simple line:** If a customer is worth AED 3,600 and costs AED 200, you're winning. If they're worth AED 300 and cost AED 200, your ads are eating your profit.
- **Visual:** A balance scale: a heavy bag of value on one side, a small price tag on the other.

### Slide 5: 4/7 · Repeat rate
- **Headline:** How many customers come back?
- **Simple line:** Getting a new customer is expensive. Keeping one is cheap.
- **Example:** 100 first-time customers → 35 return = **35% repeat rate**
- **Visual:** A revolving door, with some people coming back round.

### Slide 6: 5/7 · Reply time
- **Headline:** How fast do you answer a new message?
- **Simple line:** In the UAE people message 3–4 businesses at once. The fastest reply usually wins the booking.
- **Visual:** WhatsApp chat: "Hi, is this available?" with timestamps from 4 businesses, and a ✓ on the one that replied in 2 minutes.
- **Tip on slide:** Target: under 5 minutes.

### Slide 7: 6/7 · Enquiry → customer rate
- **Headline:** How many enquiries become paying customers?
- **Simple line:** 100 messages and 5 bookings means the problem isn't your marketing. It's what happens after the message.
- **Example:** 100 enquiries → 20 bookings = **20%**
- **Visual:** A funnel. Lots of chat bubbles go in the top; a few calendar ticks come out the bottom.

### Slide 8: 7/7 · Shares
- **Headline:** How many people sent your post to a friend?
- **Simple line:** A like is a nod. A share is a recommendation. Instagram shows shared posts to more new people.
- **Visual:** The paper-plane share icon, big, with 3 small phones receiving it.

### Slide 9: Stop obsessing over these
- **Headline:** Nice to see. Not what pays you.
- **List (crossed out):** ~~Likes~~ · ~~Followers~~ · ~~Views~~
- **Simple line:** They feel good, but they don't pay rent. Use them as clues, not goals.
- **Visual:** A scoreboard with those three words fading out.
