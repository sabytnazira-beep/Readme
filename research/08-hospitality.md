# 08: Hospitality Businesses

**Tier 2, strong potential.** Boutique hotels, luxury serviced apartments, beach clubs, premium hospitality concepts, restaurants inside hotels.

See [00-overview](00-overview-market-context.md) for the shared 2026 context.

---

## 1. Market right now
- **This segment was hit hardest in 2026.** Occupancy fell from ~85% (Jan–Feb) to ~23% in mid-March, with ~60% cancellations within 48 hours of the strikes. Some hotel restaurants suspended operations (Atlantis was reported).
- **Recovery:** occupancy was 66% in August (≈89% of Aug 2025, up from 36% in March). Emirates carried 8.7m passengers in July–August versus 4.7m in March–April. September is framed as a turning point.
- **Recovery plan:** Dubai's strategy leans on the **winter season, events calendar and repeat visitors** (including visiting friends and relatives).
- **Residents are the new key guest.** Resident rates run **15–40% below tourist pricing**, and hotels ran aggressive resident offers such as fully redeemable room credit, free pool tickets and spa or dining discounts.
- **Beach clubs** compete on free or redeemable entry, and day passes are the gateway.

## 2. What their guests are choosing
- **Residents:** staycations, day passes, brunches, spa days, family packages, all with clear value (redeemable credit).
- **Returning international guests:** reassurance, flexible cancellation, events.
- **Book direct when there's a perk**: best rate, credit, upgrade, late checkout.
- **Experience-led content**: what the day or night actually feels like.

## 3. What the owner or GM is thinking
- "We need to fill rooms and outlets now, but I don't want to become a discount brand."
- "OTAs take 15–25% and own the guest."
- "Our in-hotel restaurant is invisible to people who don't stay with us."
- "Winter season has to make up for 2026."

## 4. Main pain points
1. **Demand shock and uncertainty.**
2. **OTA dependency** and commission.
3. **Discounting versus brand positioning.**
4. **Hotel F&B outlets without their own brand identity.**
5. **Content that looks like every hotel**: drone pools, empty rooms.
6. **Measuring direct-booking impact.**

## 5. What works in marketing now
| Lever | How |
|---|---|
| **Resident campaigns** | Emirates ID offers, redeemable credit, day passes, promoted to residents |
| **Experience films** | "A day at…" with people and moments, not empty rooms |
| **Outlet-as-brand** | Give hotel restaurants and bars their own handle, chef, story and creators |
| **Event-led content** | Winter events, DJ nights, seasonal brunches |
| **Direct-booking incentives** | Content that pushes to direct channels, plus WhatsApp concierge |
| **Micro creators** | Local families, couples and lifestyle creators, permit-checked |
| **Winter international push** | Reassurance plus experience content for returning markets |

## 6. What doesn't work
- Empty-room and drone-only content.
- Relying only on OTAs.
- Blanket discounts with no value-add framing.

## 7. Example scenario (illustrative)
**A 90-key boutique hotel in Downtown with a rooftop restaurant. Occupancy is recovering but depends on OTAs, and the restaurant is quiet on weekdays.**
- **Fix:**
  1. A resident "Rooftop Nights" series with its own handle and a chef-led series.
  2. A direct-booking staycation with dining credit.
  3. Quarterly hero film for winter.
  4. 8 micro creators a month.
  5. WhatsApp concierge booking.
- **Measure:** direct booking share, outlet covers from non-residents, RevPAR, cost per direct booking.

## 8. Best offer for this segment (*Offer*)
**"Direct Demand" package:**
- Hero seasonal film and stills
- Monthly outlet and experience content
- Resident campaign management
- Creator program
- Direct-booking funnel

## 9. KPIs to report
Direct vs. OTA booking share, occupancy/RevPAR trend, outlet covers, day-pass sales, cost per direct booking.

## 10. Outreach hook
> "Residents are your most important guests in 2026. Here's how to turn them into direct bookings and a busy restaurant, without looking like a discount hotel."
