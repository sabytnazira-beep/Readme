# 12: Financial Services

**Tier 3, higher-ticket B2B/B2HNW.** Wealth management, financial advisory, insurance, mortgage advisory, investment-related businesses.

See [00-overview](00-overview-market-context.md) for the shared 2026 context.

---

## 1. Market right now
- **The UAE is the world's #1 wealth-migration destination** for the 5th year running, with a projected **~9,800 net millionaire inflow in 2026**.
- **Family offices are expanding:** 1,289+ family-related entities in DIFC and 500+ wealth and asset managers. **120+ licensed multi-family offices** across DIFC and ADGM reportedly manage ~AED 550bn. A January 2026 survey found 73% of USD 500m+ family offices plan UAE operations within 18 months. The UK non-dom abolition is the single largest driver.
- **Regulatory change:** from **1 January 2026 the SCA was replaced by the Capital Markets Authority (CMA)**. SCA-era promotion rules continue unless replaced. **Promoting financial products in the UAE requires a licence.** DIFC (DFSA) and ADGM (FSRA) have their own rules.
- **Mortgage advisory** is tied to property. Volumes fell in 2026 while prices held, so refinancing and first-time-buyer education matter more.
- **Insurance**: health and life needs keep rising with the population. Largely commoditised online.

## 2. What their clients are choosing
- **Trust, credentials and discretion** above everything.
- **Named advisors** with visible expertise and regulatory status.
- **Educational, calm content**, especially during 2026 volatility.
- **Relocation-specific help**: new arrivals need banking, structuring, estate planning, mortgages.
- **Multilingual advice** (English, Arabic, Russian, Hindi, French, etc., depending on segment).

## 3. What the owner is thinking
- "Compliance makes marketing scary. What are we even allowed to say?"
- "Leads from ads are low quality. HNW clients come from referrals and events."
- "Our brand looks like every bank: stock handshakes, skyline."
- "We need to reach newly relocated wealth before the big banks do."

## 4. Main pain points
1. **Regulatory limits** on promotion and claims (CMA/DFSA/FSRA).
2. **Trust barrier** with new clients.
3. **Lead quality** (HNW is hard to target).
4. **Generic, corporate branding.**
5. **Advisor time and compliance review cycles.**

## 5. What works in marketing now
| Lever | How |
|---|---|
| **Advisor-led education video** | "Relocating to the UAE: 5 structuring mistakes", market commentary, compliance-reviewed |
| **Premium brand photography** | Real team and real offices (discreet luxury), not stock |
| **Long-form content** | Podcasts, webinars, reports. HNW clients consume depth |
| **Private events** | Intimate dinners and briefings with content captured discreetly |
| **LinkedIn thought leadership** | Partner and advisor profiles |
| **Referral partner content** | Co-created with lawyers, real estate agents, relocation firms |
| **Mortgage: calculators + explainers** | First-time buyer and refinancing education |

## 6. What doesn't work
- Return promises or performance claims (a regulatory risk).
- Hard-sell ads and flashy "get rich" creative.
- Paid creators promoting financial products without the correct licences.

## 7. Example scenario (illustrative)
**A DIFC-based independent wealth advisory with 8 advisors targeting relocating UK and European families. Relies on referrals, has a corporate stock-photo website and no video.**
- **Fix:**
  1. Brand photography and advisor portraits.
  2. A monthly "Relocation Brief" video series, compliance-reviewed before publishing.
  3. A quarterly private briefing event with content capture.
  4. A LinkedIn advisor program.
  5. Co-branded content with 2 relocation law firms.
- **Measure:** qualified discovery calls, AUM from new clients, event-to-meeting conversion.

## 8. Best offer for this segment (*Offer*)
**"Trust Engine":** premium brand photography and film, an advisor education series produced around a compliance-review workflow, event content, and a LinkedIn program. Emphasise discretion and a compliance-aware process. Leave regulatory sign-off to the client's compliance team.

## 9. KPIs to report
Qualified discovery calls, meeting-to-client conversion, new AUM or policies influenced, event attendance to meetings, referral-partner leads.

## 10. Outreach hook
> "Nearly 10,000 millionaires are moving to the UAE this year, and they choose advisors they've already seen explain things clearly. Here's a compliance-friendly way to be that advisor."
