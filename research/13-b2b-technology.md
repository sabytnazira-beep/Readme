# 13: B2B Technology Companies

**Tier 3, higher-ticket B2B.** SaaS, software companies, IT providers, business automation, cybersecurity.

See [00-overview](00-overview-market-context.md) for the shared 2026 context.

---

## 1. Market right now
- **The UAE ICT market** is ~USD 57.6bn in 2026, heading to ~USD 94bn by 2031 (~10.3% CAGR), driven by digital transformation and smart-city programmes.
- **Cybersecurity** is ~USD 0.91bn, heading to ~USD 1.51bn by 2031 (~10.7% CAGR). There is a **~30% shortage of certified cybersecurity talent**, which pushes demand for managed security services.
- The **UAE National AI Strategy 2031** pushes AI, data and automation adoption across operations and customer functions. "AI" is the headline buying theme.
- **UAE B2B buyers respond to named, local, evidence-rich content.** A **bilingual two-page case study** with the client's logo, a named quote and a quantified result is the single highest-converting asset.
- Government and semi-government buyers are a big share of the market, with long procurement cycles and relationship-driven sales.

## 2. What their buyers are choosing
- **Local proof.** "Who in the UAE uses this?"
- **Vendors with a local presence**: team, support, data residency.
- **Clear ROI** and demos over feature lists.
- **AI that is practical**, not hype.
- **Trust and security credentials** (especially for cybersecurity).

## 3. What the founder or CMO is thinking
- "Our product is strong but we look like every other SaaS: blue gradients, abstract illustrations."
- "Global HQ content doesn't work locally."
- "Sales cycles are long. We need pipeline, not likes."
- "We have no content showing our UAE team and customers."

## 4. Main pain points
1. **Undifferentiated, abstract branding.**
2. **No local proof** (case studies, testimonials).
3. **Long, relationship-heavy sales cycles.**
4. **Explaining complex products simply.**
5. **Events are expensive**, and without content capture most of their value is lost afterwards.

## 5. What works in marketing now
| Lever | How |
|---|---|
| **Customer case-study films** | Named UAE client, on-site, quantified outcome, bilingual. Your core offer |
| **Founder and expert video** | LinkedIn POV on AI, security and automation |
| **Product explainers** | Short, human-led demos rather than animation-only |
| **Event content capture** | GITEX, conferences, roundtables turned into a month of content |
| **Team and culture content** | Local presence proof and hiring (cyber talent is scarce) |
| **LinkedIn ABM ads** | Targeted by company and job title, promoting case studies |

## 6. What doesn't work
- Generic global content with no local relevance.
- Stock imagery and abstract tech visuals only.
- Trend-chasing consumer content.

## 7. Example scenario (illustrative)
**A Dubai-based business automation SaaS, Series A, selling to mid-market companies. Strong product, weak brand, no local case studies, and a big GITEX spend with little follow-up content.**
- **Fix:**
  1. 3 customer case-study films per quarter.
  2. Monthly founder POV videos.
  3. Full GITEX content capture (booth interviews, customer clips).
  4. Team photography.
  5. A LinkedIn ABM campaign promoting the case studies.
- **Measure:** meetings booked, pipeline influenced, content-assisted deals, case-study engagement from target accounts.

## 8. Best offer for this segment (*Offer*)
**"Proof & Pipeline":** quarterly case-study films, event capture packages, a founder/expert video program, and brand photography. High-ticket, project-based and retainer mix.

## 9. KPIs to report
Meetings booked, pipeline influenced or sourced, target-account engagement, demo requests, win-rate lift on content-assisted deals.

## 10. Outreach hook
> "UAE buyers want to know who else here uses you. One filmed customer story will do more for your pipeline than a year of abstract ads."
