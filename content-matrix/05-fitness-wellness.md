| Person | Profile | Current state | Sends content to | What they buy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Studio founder** (primary) | Pilates, boutique gym, recovery/longevity; 1–3 locations | Classes full on intro offers, weak conversion to membership; new competitors nearby | Co-founder, head coach | Content plus onboarding/retention system |

| **Pre-opening founder** | Launching in 1–6 months | Needs founding members before opening | Investor, designer | Launch package |
