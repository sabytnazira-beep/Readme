## 1. Right person

| Person | Profile | Current state | Sends content to | What they buy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Brokerage founder / MD** (primary) | 10–60 agents, luxury/premium Dubai | Volume down, portal leads poor, brand looks generic | Partners, sales director | Founder authority content plus pipeline system |
| **Head of marketing** | Big budget, portal-heavy | Can't prove ROI, listings-only feed | MD | Production capacity, listing films, nurture |
| **Top agent** (secondary) | Personal brand ambitions | Wants their own inbound leads | Other agents, MD | Personal-brand program (often paid by the agency) |
