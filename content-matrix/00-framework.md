# Instagram Content Matrix: Framework

**RIGHT PERSON × RIGHT CONTENT × RIGHT SIGNAL × RIGHT NEXT ACTION**

This folder has one matrix per target segment. Each file names the exact buyer, maps each post to the Instagram signal it's built to earn, and connects that signal to a next action. The market research behind each segment is in [`/research`](../research/00-overview-market-context.md).

---

## 1. What Instagram rewards in 2026 (the signals we design for)

| Signal | What it does | Why it matters for us |
|---|---|---|
| **Sends per reach** (DM shares) | The strongest signal for reaching **non-followers**. Instagram leadership has reportedly said sends weigh roughly 3–5× more than likes for non-follower distribution | Share content is how we reach buyers who don't know us yet |
| **Watch time / retention** | The most important signal across surfaces. Reels are tested on a small pool first, and distribution expands if watch time and sends are strong | Watch content earns the second round of distribution |
| **Saves** | Tells Instagram the content has lasting value. Also keeps it in the buyer's own library | Save content builds expertise and brings the buyer back |
| **Likes per reach** | Matters mostly for reach to **existing followers** | A health check, not a goal |
| **Profile visits → follows** | Shows the account is worth following, not just one post | Profile content has to convert that visit |
| **DMs** | The conversion point. Keyword-triggered DMs are measurable | Where the pipeline starts |

**Other 2026 rules:**
- **Originality.** Since 30 April 2026, accounts that mostly post content they didn't create are not recommended to non-followers, and this now covers photos and carousels too. Everything we publish must be original footage.
- **Keywords over hashtags.** Instagram search runs on caption text, on-screen text, alt text and profile keywords. Use 3–5 relevant hashtags at most, and write captions with the exact phrases a buyer would search.
- **Trial Reels.** Test hooks on non-followers first (4–7 a week), then publish the winners to the main feed.

## 2. The signal ladder

```
SHARES  →  SAVES  →  WATCHES  →  PROFILE VISITS  →  DMs
(reach)    (value)   (depth)     (consideration)    (conversion)
```

| Level | Share of posts | Goal | Built for | Primary signal | Next action (CTA) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **SHARE** | **40%** | Reach non-followers | A statement the buyer wants to send to a colleague, partner or co-founder | Sends per reach, 3-second hold | "Send this to your ___" |
| **SAVE** | **25%** | Establish expertise | A framework, breakdown or checklist | Saves per reach | "Save this for your next ___" |
| **WATCH** | **20%** | Build depth and trust | A story with a progressive reveal | Average watch time, rewatches, completion | "Part 2 on our profile" / follow |
| **PROFILE** | **10%** | Convert the visit | Proof: real shoots, boards, systems, results | Profile visits → follows | Pinned posts and highlights |
| **CONVERT** | **5%** | Start conversations | A direct offer | DMs (keyword) | "DM AUDIT" → Growth Audit |

**Monthly cadence (20 feed posts):** 8 share / 5 save / 4 watch / 2 profile / 1 convert, plus daily Stories that retarget warm viewers.

Each segment file has **10 master posts** (4 share / 2 save / 2 watch / 1 profile / 1 convert). Fill the 20-post month by testing 2–3 hook variants of the share and save masters as Trial Reels.

## 3. The per-post matrix (fill all cells before publishing)

| Cell | Question it answers |
|---|---|
| **TARGET PERSON** | Exactly who should see this? (role, segment, size) |
| **CURRENT STATE** | What are they struggling with or believing right now? |
| **HOOK** | First 1–2 seconds, both on-screen and spoken |
| **RETENTION MECHANISM** | Why they keep watching (reveal, list, before/after of the *process*, story, contrast) |
| **IG SIGNAL** | The main signal this post is built to earn |
| **PROFILE BRIDGE** | The line that makes them tap the profile ("we work as an extension of marketing teams") |
| **COMMERCIAL BRIDGE** | The package this post eventually leads to |
| **NEXT ACTION** | The single CTA |

## 4. Helping Instagram find the right person

Instagram decides who to show a post to based on what's in it and who reacts first. Make every post easy to classify:

1. **Say the segment out loud.** Put it in the on-screen text in the first 2 seconds ("Coffee brand owners…", "Clinic marketing managers…"). The right person stops scrolling and the wrong person scrolls past, which trains the test pool.
2. **Caption keywords.** Open the caption with the buyer's own search phrase ("specialty coffee marketing Dubai", "restaurant marketing UAE"). Add alt text.
3. **3–5 hashtags maximum**, segment and location specific.
4. **Collab posts** with clients (the client as co-author) put the post in front of the client's followers, who are usually their peers and competitors.
5. **Tag locations** in Dubai and Abu Dhabi business districts that match the segment.
6. **Early engagement from the right people.** Share new posts to your Close Friends list of prospects and to WhatsApp groups of owners in the segment.
7. **Comment-to-DM keywords** (e.g. "Comment CALENDAR"). These drive comments and DMs and can be tracked by keyword.

## 5. One account, many segments: focus rule

If the account posts for 14 segments at once, Instagram can't work out who the audience is and every segment gets weak reach.

- **Choose 1 primary and 1 secondary segment per quarter.** About 70% of posts go to the primary, 20% to the secondary, and 10% are cross-segment (agency proof and behind the scenes).
- Suggested order, based on the research: **Q4 2026: specialty coffee (primary) + restaurants (secondary)**, since winter season urgency and visual fit are both strongest there. **Q1 2027: clinics + interior design.**
- Use a **named series per segment** (e.g. "Coffee Brand Teardown") so series content becomes recognisable and bingeable.

## 6. Measurement (weekly)

| Level | Metric | How to read it |
|---|---|---|
| Share | Sends ÷ reach; non-follower reach % | Rising non-follower reach means the hook is travelling |
| Save | Saves ÷ reach | Your expertise signal |
| Watch | Average watch time, % watched, 3-second hold | Fix hooks if the 3-second hold is low; fix the middle if completion is low |
| Profile | Profile visits ÷ reach; follows ÷ profile visits | A low follow rate means the profile doesn't explain the offer |
| Convert | DMs by keyword → audits booked → clients | The pipeline |

Set your own baseline from the first 4 weeks, then aim to beat it each month. Published "benchmarks" vary too much to rely on.
