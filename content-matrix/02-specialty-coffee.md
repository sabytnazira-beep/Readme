Research: [02-specialty-coffee-premium-cafes](../research/02-specialty-coffee-premium-cafes.md) · Framework: [00-framework](00-framework.md)
Commercial offer: **"Content Engine" retainer** (plus a launch package for new openings) → entry point: **Growth Audit**

## 1. Right person

| Person | Profile | Current state | Sends content to | What they buy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Marketing manager** (primary, multi-branch) | 3–10 branches, 3–5+ years old, in-house team of 1–3 | Has strategy, lacks execution capacity. The team copies trends, the account is stagnating | Head of marketing, founder, their team | Production capacity and a playbook ("extension of the team") |
| **Founder** (independent, 1–3 branches) | Design-led brand, founder still hands-on | "Clean cafés aren't fun anymore, but I won't lose our premium feel" | Co-founder, head barista | Brand voice refresh plus monthly content |
| **New-opening founder** | Opening in 1–6 months | Needs demand from day 1 in the densest café market in the GCC | Investor/partner, fit-out designer | Launch package |
