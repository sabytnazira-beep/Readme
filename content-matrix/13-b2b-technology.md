# Content Matrix 13: B2B Technology

Research: [13-b2b-technology](../research/13-b2b-technology.md) · Framework: [00-framework](00-framework.md)
Commercial offer: **"Proof & Pipeline"** → entry point: **Growth Audit (local proof review)**

> **Channel note:** LinkedIn is primary and Instagram supports it. On Instagram, focus on founders and marketing leads of UAE tech companies. Many of them follow startup and ecosystem accounts (Hub71, Dubai Future Foundation, in5).

## 1. Right person

| Person | Profile | Current state | Sends content to | What they buy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Head of marketing / CMO** (primary) | SaaS, IT services, automation, cybersecurity; 20–300 people, UAE HQ or regional office | Abstract branding, no local case studies, big event spend with little follow-up | CEO, sales director | Case-study films plus event capture |
| **Founder / CEO** | Series A–C or bootstrapped | Needs pipeline, wants to be the category voice | Co-founder, board | Founder video program |
| **Regional marketing manager** (global vendor) | Global content that doesn't land locally | Needs local proof | Regional VP | Localised case studies |

## 2. Distribution setup
- **On-screen callout:** "B2B tech marketers in the UAE…", "If you sell software in the Gulf…"
- **Caption keywords:** B2B marketing UAE, SaaS marketing Dubai, customer case study video, GITEX content, tech brand video
- **Hashtags:** #b2bmarketing #saasmarketing #dubaitech #gitex #uaestartups
- **Collabs:** client tech companies, ecosystem hubs (Hub71, in5, DIFC Innovation Hub), event organisers
- **Locations:** Dubai Internet City, DIFC Innovation Hub, Hub71 (Abu Dhabi), Dubai World Trade Centre (GITEX)
- **DM keywords:** `CASESTUDY` (bilingual case-study template), `AUDIT`
