Research: [12-financial-services](../research/12-financial-services.md) · Framework: [00-framework](00-framework.md)
Commercial offer: **"Trust Engine"** → entry point: **Growth Audit (brand trust review)**

> **Compliance note:** your agency posts talk about *marketing* for financial firms and must never promote financial products. Client content goes through the **client's compliance team** (CMA / DFSA / FSRA rules). Never show performance claims or return figures.
>
> **Channel note:** LinkedIn is primary for this segment and Instagram supports it. HNW relocators *do* use Instagram, which makes it a stronger channel here for the advisor's own brand than for your agency's.
