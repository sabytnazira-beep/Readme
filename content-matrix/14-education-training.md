# Content Matrix 14: Education & Training

Research: [14-education-training](../research/14-education-training.md) · Framework: [00-framework](00-framework.md)
Commercial offer: **"Enrolment Engine"** → entry point: **Growth Audit (enquiry-to-enrolment review)**

> **Channel note:** Instagram works well for B2C courses (career changers, certifications). For executive education and corporate training, LinkedIn is primary and Instagram supports it.

## 1. Right person

| Person | Profile | Current state | Sends content to | What they buy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Institute owner / director** (primary) | KHDA-approved training institute: AI, data, PM, languages, professional certifications | Google Ads dependent, low enquiry-to-enrolment, competing with 1,000+ institutes | Academic head, sales team | Trainer and alumni content plus enrolment funnel |
| **Marketing / admissions manager** | Owns leads | Leads are price shoppers, slow follow-up | Director | WhatsApp enrolment flow, webinar content |
| **Executive education / corporate training director** | Business school or consulting academy | Long HR sales cycles, needs ROI proof | Faculty, BD | Corporate case studies, faculty content |
