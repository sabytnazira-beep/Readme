# Content Matrix 03: Premium Clinics (Derm, Aesthetic, Dental, Wellness)

Research: [03-premium-clinics](../research/03-premium-clinics.md) · Framework: [00-framework](00-framework.md)
Commercial offer: **"Compliant Patient Engine"** → entry point: **Growth Audit (lead-leak + compliance check)**

> **Compliance note for *your own* posts:** showing clinic client work counts as medical advertising for the client. Only use client footage that is DHA/DoH-approved and consented, never before/after patient images, and never edited results. Your agency content should show **process and systems**, not treatment outcomes.
