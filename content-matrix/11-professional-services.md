# Content Matrix 11: Professional Services

Research: [11-professional-services](../research/11-professional-services.md) · Framework: [00-framework](00-framework.md)
Commercial offer: **"Authority Engine"** → entry point: **Growth Audit (partner visibility review)**

> **B2B channel note:** for this segment **LinkedIn is the primary channel** and Instagram supports it (founders and partners in Dubai do scroll Instagram). Cut every post for both platforms: Reels and carousels on Instagram, native video and document posts on LinkedIn. On LinkedIn, "sends" become reposts and comments, but the same ladder logic applies.

## 1. Right person

| Person | Profile | Current state | Sends content to | What they buy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Managing partner / founder** (primary) | Law, accounting/tax, consultancy, corporate services, recruitment; 5–80 people | 80–90% referral-driven, invisible online, no time | Other partners | Partner filming sessions plus LinkedIn system |
| **Marketing / BD manager** | Small team, approval bottlenecks | Content stuck in partner review | Managing partner | Batch production plus workflow |
| **Recruitment firm founder** | Candidate and client brand | Employer-brand content gap | Consultants | Culture and authority content |
